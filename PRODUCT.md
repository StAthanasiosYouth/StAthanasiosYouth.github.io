# Product

## What it is

The official digital home of **أسرة البابا أثناسيوس لخدمة شعب كنيستها
الحبيبة في سفاجا**: one permanent link that answers what a young person
needs from the youth meeting, in seconds.

register: brand (the page is the product's face), with a product-style
admin behind it.

## Who it's for

- **Youth attending the meeting (المخدومين)**, mostly on mid-range Android
  phones, arriving from a QR code, WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok, often in
  the evening, often on mobile data.
- Not for servants: servant-only tools (like the meeting-topics suggestion
  portal) never appear here.

## What they come for (priority order)

1. Recognize it's ours: logo, official name.
2. When is the next meeting? ("النهارده", "بكره", "فاضل ٣ أيام")
3. Anything special this week? (announcement)
4. صوتك يهمنا: a real service, so it gets the strongest card.
5. Where is it? (map, directions)
6. Our pages (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).
7. Who do I call? (أمين الخدمة, phone only)
8. The site doesn't work? (technical support, WhatsApp only, deliberately quieter)
9. Share it with a friend (share sheet, QR).

## Voice

Egyptian Arabic, warm and short, like a servant talking to a youth, not a
notice board. Arabic-Indic numerals in prose; phone numbers in Western
digits so they're easy to dial.

## Success

- The link is the one we print and put in bios, and nobody asks "what time
  is the meeting?" in the group anymore.
- Useful content visible within about a second of a QR scan on 4G.
- The admin can change anything without touching code, from a phone.

## Anti-goals

- A generic Linktree clone (identical stacked buttons).
- A SaaS dashboard look.
- A copy of صوتك يهمنا. Same family, different product.
- Loading screens that make people wait, autoplay sound, trackers. (The short intro
  is not one: the real page is there underneath from the first moment, any tap
  skips it, and it plays once per session.)
